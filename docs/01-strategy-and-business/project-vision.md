# Project Vision — PV-001
**State:** Proposed | **Owner:** Architecture | **Revision:** 0.1

## Problem
Enterprises manage batch and real-time data in fragmented systems. Analysts lack shared provenance, governed AI access, and reliable operations. AI assistants can hallucinate, mishandle access control or act beyond approved scope.

## Vision
A multilingual enterprise intelligence platform that converts permissioned data streams into timely, explainable and auditable insights, with humans controlling consequential actions.

## Stakeholders
Data platform engineers; analytics and ML teams; enterprise architects; information security; compliance; operational reviewers; business executives; cloud operations.

## Success outcomes
- Complete aviation vertical slice with real external inputs, provenance and visible event health.
- Reproducible event delivery, schema checks, deduplication and replay tests.
- Grounded AI investigations with abstention and access enforcement.
- Demonstrated restoration, rollback, monitoring and cost records.
- Published sanitized evidence, not unverifiable scale or uptime claims.

## Scope boundaries
V1 is a portfolio-operated research/development deployment; it is NOT an air traffic control system or a production aviation safety decision-maker. No automatic operational intervention. Personal information, licensed data and private integrations are excluded until approved.
